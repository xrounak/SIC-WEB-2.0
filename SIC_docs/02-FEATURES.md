# 02 — Features and Requirements

Priority key: P0 = launch blocker; P1 = important after core launch; P2 = later enhancement.

## Public website
- P0: Home page with upcoming events, latest updates, achievements and project highlights.
- P0: About SIC, mission, leadership, domains, teams, contact and social links.
- P0: Events list, event detail, registration form and confirmation.
- P0: Projects/achievements gallery with descriptions, domains, contributors and media.
- P0: Join SIC / recruitment information and application form.
- P1: Resources and workshop materials.
- P1: Sponsors and partners.
- P1: Curated engineering/science news with source links and SIC-written articles.
- P2: Search, filtering, bookmarks and advanced discovery.

## Authentication and profiles
- P0: Sign in, sign out, password reset, session handling.
- P0: Member profile with name, college email, year, branch and optional bio/avatar.
- P0: Private-by-default contact details.
- P0: Role-aware navigation and server-side/database authorization.
- P1: Invite-only member activation and account deactivation flow.

## Domains and teams
- P0: Domains include Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, Electronics, Robotics, IoT, AI/ML, Software, Research, Media, Management, PR and configurable additions.
- P0: Members can belong to many domains and teams.
- P0: Leads can manage only teams/domains assigned to them.
- P1: Team roster, responsibilities and contribution history.

## Events
- P0: Event proposal, review, approve/reject, publish, cancel and archive.
- P0: Event categories: workshop, fest/hackathon, competition, seminar/guest lecture, external/inter-college event, internal meeting.
- P0: Public registration without account, with validation and duplicate prevention.
- P0: Registration list available only to authorized organizers.
- P1: Capacity, waitlist, attendance, QR check-in and manual check-in.
- P1: Email confirmation and reminders.
- P2: Calendar sync and recurring events.

## Projects
- P0: Submit project with title, summary, domain, status, contributors and media.
- P0: Project review and publication status.
- P0: Showcase hardware/prototype details and outcomes.
- P1: Progress updates, repository/demo links and project milestones.
- P2: Public project submission portal for non-members, if approved by club.

## Recruitment
- P0: Application form for any eligible student from the college.
- P0: Status stages: submitted, screening, shortlisted, interview, selected, rejected, waitlisted, withdrawn.
- P0: Private evaluation notes and decision history.
- P1: Interview scheduling and email updates.
- P1: Configurable questions by recruitment cycle.

## Competitions
- P1: Create competition, allow individual/team entries, record results and leaderboard.
- P1: Certificate issue tracking and downloadable certificates.
- P2: Automated certificate generation and verification links.

## Attendance and contributions
- P1: QR check-in with short-lived or event-scoped token, plus manual attendance.
- P1: Record attendance actor, timestamp and method.
- P1: Contribution records for projects/events/operations.
- P2: Reports and configurable contribution scoring. Do not equate raw attendance with performance without an explicit club policy.

## Announcements and notifications
- P0: Public announcements.
- P0: Internal member notices.
- P1: In-app notification center.
- P1: Email confirmations and important updates.
- P2: User notification preferences and scheduled digests.

## Admin and governance
- P0: Role assignment restricted to trusted administrators.
- P0: Approval audit trail.
- P0: Basic counts and operational dashboard.
- P1: CSV export for authorized staff, with privacy controls.
- P1: Audit log and archive tools.
